Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25. Version 1.0 of the app.

Scanya Ticket is built so that we never hold information about you. This policy says what that means in practice.

The short version

  • We do not collect your data. Everything you scan stays on your device.
  • We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • We never sell or share your data, because we do not have it.

What the app does with your information

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Photos and files of receipts you scan or importTo read the items and pricesRead on your device with Apple's text recognition. Stored only on your device.
Receipt contents, categories, groups, your classification progressTo show your spending and run the daily gameStored only on your device.
Your voice, if you describe a category by speakingTo turn speech into textConverted to text on your device only. Nothing is sent to a server.
Category suggestions and receipt checksTo suggest categories and spot reading mistakesDone by Apple Intelligence on your device, when your device supports it.
Daily reminder, if you allow itTo nudge you to classify your itemsA local notification scheduled on your device.

Permissions we ask for

  • Camera: to scan receipts.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: only if you use voice to describe a category.
  • Notifications: only if you say yes to the daily reminder. Saying no changes nothing else.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in iOS Settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not track you, across apps or websites.
  • We do not use third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or attribution.
  • We do not create an account or identifier for you.
  • Diagnostic events stay on your device and contain no free text.

Deleting your data

Account > Delete all my data removes your receipts, categories, groups and progress from your device. Deleting the app removes everything as well. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the way the app handles data ever changes, for example if you are offered an optional feature that shares anything, we will update this policy first, ask for your explicit consent in the app, and keep any such feature off by default.
