Common questions

A receipt was not read correctly.
Retake the photo in good light, flat and fully in frame. You can also correct items by hand on the receipt screen.

The daily reminder does not appear.
Turn on notifications for Scanya Ticket in iOS Settings.

Voice category does not work.
Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in iOS Settings. Speech is processed on your device.

Category suggestions are missing.
They need Apple Intelligence, which is available only on supported devices. The rest of the app works without it.

How do I delete my data?
Account > Delete all my data. Everything is stored on your device only.

Contact

Write to support@lingress.org. Please do not attach receipts that show personal or card details.
